Three problems I'm brought in to solve:

“We're growing, service is slipping, and we can't hire our way out.”
I redesign how the team works and what tools it uses, so each person can serve more clients well. At Segmint, clients grew 725% while the team grew 230%, at 99% retention. Repeated at Alkami: clients up 40% on a 23% headcount increase. Case study →
“We've invested in AI and the work hasn't changed.”
I find the manual, error-prone steps and put AI agents into the live process. AI handles synthesis and drafting; people own judgment and decisions. 30+ agents in production. Case study →
“We bought a company and the two still don't run as one.”
I run the integration office: one plan, a named owner for each handoff, visible risks. Most recently for a $400M acquisition, one of three integrations I've worked on. The method →

I did this at Segmint, a marketing and customer-data platform for banks and credit unions, through its $135MM exit to Alkami Technology in 2022, and then at Alkami (NASDAQ: ALKT), whose digital banking platform serves more than 300 U.S. banks and credit unions and 24 million users.

The edge on all three: I've done it in regulated financial services, where legal, compliance, product and sales all have to agree before anything ships.
