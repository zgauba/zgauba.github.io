## What it does

A new read-only function, `jc_my_bookings_past()`, callable by `authenticated` only. `jc_my_bookings()` is unchanged.

- **Scope.** The caller's own home, ended slots. Booked rows ("Done") and organizer-cancelled rows (with their
  reason). The household's own cancels are left out.
- **Window.** Only bookings **made after this member joined**, with the slot in the last 90 days (the retention job
  deletes rows at 90 days).
- **No names or people.** It returns the court, times, status and reason only.
- **Order and size.** Newest first, at most 100.
- **Gates.** `MODULE_OFF` when Courts is off; `[]` without a home.

## Security review: one Medium (fixed), three Lows

| # | Sev | Finding | Disposition |

| L2 | L | The status filter matched a reason on a non-cancelled row | **Fixed.** `status = 'cancelled' and cancel_reason is not null` |
| L3 | L | Test gaps: real sale flow, PUBLIC grant, newest-100 kept | **Fixed.** P10, P09b, P08b/c. Not added: `NOT_MEMBER` / signed-out (covered by `jc_me` in other slices) |

The reviewer verified:
- **Identity.** No parameter; identity comes from `jc_me()` / `auth.uid()`.
- **`joined_at` can't be backdated.** It is always `now()` on insert, never updated by any function, and there is no
  direct table access.
- **Re-joining only hides history** (fail closed).
- **`search_path` is pinned.**
- **Data minimization holds.**
