The reviewer checked and found sound:
- **Time zone:** today, times and day names come from `cfg.tz` or UTC noon, and block ranges reuse the server's own instants.
- **The 30-day bound:** it stays under the server's 31.
- **The busy flag:** it resets on every path of the two-step multi-day flow.
- **Selects:** `followStart` index handling on the selects.
- **XSS:** every string goes through `esc()`.
- **Draft restore:** the form is restored before wiring, on a language switch.
