Proof

What it looks like in practice.

Partner onboarding: 205 to 90 business days
Onboarding ran off a checklist, with one person chasing other teams for favors and nobody able to see what was at risk. I turned it into a shared schedule with a named owner for every task. Any delay showed its effect on the launch date at once, so priority calls became short and factual. Replacing manual order forms with self-serve dashboards freed another 90 hours a month across legal, deal desk and finance. Case study →
Segmint: 20 to 165 clients at 99% retention
A startup can't add a client success manager for every new batch of clients. I split the job in two: senior managers kept only the client relationship, and one shared client care team took the admin for all of them. Before every hire I asked what it would take for a system to do this instead of a person. Client care became the route for junior hires to grow into managers. Case study →
AI that catches contract errors at signing
Prices and quantities that changed during negotiation weren't reaching the system of record, and errors surfaced only at billing. I put an AI agent into deal approval that read each signed contract and compared it with the system, so every difference was visible before final approval. Case study →
